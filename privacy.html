Privacy Policy

project-devops is a personal automation tool used to manage job-search activities, prepare drafts, and send application emails through configured external services.

Information we process

The app may process information needed to support the user’s own job-search workflow, including:

  • Candidate profile details and work experience
  • Application notes and status tracking
  • Recruiter or employer contact email addresses
  • Generated cover-letter content and related application data

How the information is used

This information is used to prepare, review, and send application emails, track opportunities, and help the user stay organized during their job-search process. The app is designed for individual personal use.

Storage and security

Data is stored locally on the machine running the application or in local configured data stores managed by the user. The app does not read a user’s Gmail inbox. It uses the Gmail send-only scope to send email messages and does not request broader mailbox access.

Third-party services

The app may use Google services such as Gmail and Google Sheets for sending messages and tracking application data, as configured by the user. These services are used only for the user’s own workflow and are not used to monitor unrelated accounts or communications.
