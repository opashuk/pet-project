Privacy Policy

project-devops is a personal automation app for managing job-search workflows.

Information we collect

The app may process information needed to submit job applications, including:

  • Candidate profile details
  • Cover-letter content generated for a job application
  • Recruiter or hiring contact email addresses
  • Application status and notes used for tracking

How we use the information

We use this information to prepare and send application emails, track job opportunities, and manage the job-search workflow for the user of this application.

Storage and security

Data is stored locally on the machine running the application and in configured local data stores. We do not use the Gmail API to read the user’s mailbox; the app uses the Gmail send-only scope to send emails only.

Third parties

The app may use Google services such as Gmail and Google Sheets for application delivery and spreadsheet tracking, as configured by the user.
