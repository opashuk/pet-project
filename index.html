project-devops

project-devops is a personal job-search automation app built to streamline application tracking, draft tailored cover letters, and send outreach emails through Gmail.

The app is used by its owner to manage job applications, organize candidate information, and automate routine outreach tasks in a secure local workflow.

This project is operated by its developer for personal productivity and job-search support. It is not a public SaaS product and is used for a single user’s workflow.
